About

A Riverside Escape

Georgetown Waterfront Park stretches along the Potomac River just below the historic streets of Georgetown, offering open lawns, a paved riverside promenade, and sweeping views of the water, Key Bridge, and the Virginia shoreline beyond.

Once the site of industrial warehouses and shipping activity tied to the C&O Canal, the area was transformed into public parkland, giving residents and visitors a place to walk, relax, and enjoy the river that shaped the neighborhood's history.

Today the park connects seamlessly to the Capital Crescent Trail and the C&O Canal towpath, making it a popular link in the city's network of walking and biking paths.

Things to Do

Ways to Spend Your Visit

🚶

Riverside Walking Path

Stroll the paved promenade with unobstructed views across the Potomac toward Rosslyn and Key Bridge.

🚴

Biking & Trail Access

Connect directly to the Capital Crescent Trail and C&O Canal towpath for longer rides in either direction.

⛲

Fountains & Labyrinth

Cool off near the park's fountains or take a moment at the stone labyrinth built into the landscaping.

🛶

Boathouses Nearby

Nearby boathouses offer kayak, canoe, and paddleboard rentals for getting out onto the water.

🐕

Dog-Friendly Areas

Leashed dogs are welcome throughout the park, with plenty of open lawn space to stretch their legs.

🌅

Sunset Views

One of the best spots in the city to watch the sun set over the river — arrive early to grab a bench.

Plan Your Trip

Location

Along K Street / Water Street NW, between Wisconsin Avenue and Key Bridge, Georgetown, Washington, D.C.

Hours

Open daily, dawn to dusk.

Getting There

Best reached by foot, bike, bus, or the Georgetown-area water taxi landing. Limited street parking nearby; Metro's closest stations are a walk or short ride away.

Managed By

National Park Service, as part of the C&O Canal National Historical Park system.
